# BrightMedia Hybrid Working Interview Notes  
**Stakeholder:** COO / Office Manager  
**Topic:** Hybrid working transition, desk booking, collaboration, and operational concerns

---

## Cleaned Interview Notes

### 1) Common problems or complaints about hybrid working
The most common complaints are:

- Employees lose a lot of time on commuting days.
- It is much harder to find people in person when they need to ask questions.
- Many people feel shy or hesitant about coming into the office because they are not guaranteed a desk.
- There is uncertainty about whether office attendance will be worthwhile if colleagues are not there or if space is unavailable.

---

### 2) Impact on productivity, morale, and trust in the process
These issues are having a noticeable impact on the workplace:

- The process of working has become slower because people are less comfortable asking questions.
- Workplace relationships have been affected.
- It is harder for colleagues to get to know their team members.
- Team members feel more shy and less confident interacting with each other.
- There is currently no clear solution in place, and the organisation is still unsure how best to address these challenges.

---

### 3) Security, privacy, and legal concerns
Current concerns include:

- Security officers are involved in overseeing security-related matters.
- The company needs to follow GDPR rules, but more training is needed.
- The current database is not considered very secure.
- People should be able to see when desks or rooms are booked, but not necessarily who made the booking unless they are an admin or the booking belongs to them.

---

### 4) Monitoring attendance, collaboration, and workload
Current observations include:

- Admin staff use the new setup to see who is coming in and going from their own team.
- Team cohesion is a challenge in the hybrid model.
- People appear more shy and disconnected.
- Work processes are slower when colleagues are distributed across different locations.
- Hiring talent from a wider geographic area has created both advantages and disadvantages.

---

### 5) Handling exceptions and operational risks
Current exception handling is mostly manual and creates frustration:

- Last-minute cancellations, no-shows, and double bookings waste resources.
- These issues also waste admin time because they are handled manually.
- Accessibility needs are an important concern.
- Some employees have mobility issues.
- Screen reader support is needed.
- Some rooms are client-specific and may require priority booking.

---

# Thematic Insights

## Theme 1: Uncertainty and confidence in coming into the office
**Insights**
- Employees are hesitant to commute if they are not guaranteed a desk.
- Uncertainty around desk availability reduces confidence in the hybrid working model.
- Some people feel shy about attending the office, especially if they are unsure who else will be there.

**Why it matters**
- If employees cannot rely on having a desk, they may avoid the office altogether.
- This reduces the value of in-person collaboration and weakens trust in the booking process.

---

## Theme 2: Collaboration and team cohesion are suffering
**Insights**
- It is harder for people to find colleagues and ask quick questions.
- Teams are less comfortable with one another than before.
- Workplace relationships have weakened.
- It is harder for new or less confident employees to get to know their team.

**Why it matters**
- Informal collaboration is becoming harder.
- Work is slower because people are less likely to ask for help quickly.
- Hybrid working is affecting both team cohesion and day-to-day productivity.

---

## Theme 3: Productivity is being reduced by friction in the working model
**Insights**
- Commute time feels wasted when the office experience is unpredictable.
- Work processes are slower because communication is less natural.
- Manual processes around bookings and exceptions are consuming admin time.

**Why it matters**
- Employees and admins are both losing time.
- Operational inefficiencies are reducing the benefits of hybrid working.

---

## Theme 4: Booking operations are manual and inefficient
**Insights**
- Cancellations, no-shows, and double bookings are handled manually.
- Manual handling causes frustration and wastes resources.
- Admin staff are spending time resolving issues that could potentially be automated.

**Why it matters**
- Operational overhead is high.
- The current process does not scale well.
- Booking errors directly affect employee experience and office efficiency.

---

## Theme 5: Access control, privacy, and GDPR need stronger handling
**Insights**
- The business must comply with GDPR.
- Staff need more training on privacy and data handling.
- The current database is not seen as secure enough.
- Visibility of booking information should be role-based:
  - users can see when something is booked,
  - but not necessarily who booked it,
  - unless they are an admin or viewing their own booking.

**Why it matters**
- Booking data contains employee-related information and needs controlled access.
- Privacy and security rules need to be reflected clearly in the system design.

---

## Theme 6: Accessibility and special booking needs must be supported
**Insights**
- Accessibility needs are a significant operational concern.
- Some employees have mobility-related requirements.
- Screen reader support is needed.
- Some meeting rooms may need priority access for client-related use.

**Why it matters**
- A fair booking system must support accessibility requirements properly.
- Some resources cannot be treated as first-come, first-served in all cases.

---

## Theme 7: Hybrid working creates both risks and opportunities
**Insights**
- Having talent distributed across different locations brings advantages and disadvantages.
- The organisation can hire from a wider area, but collaboration becomes harder.
- Team cohesion and comfort levels are harder to maintain in a distributed setup.

**Why it matters**
- The booking system is only one part of a broader hybrid working challenge.
- Operational design needs to support both flexibility and connection.

---

# Summary of Key Takeaways
- Employees need more certainty before commuting into the office.
- The biggest human impact is reduced collaboration, confidence, and team cohesion.
- Manual booking processes are frustrating and inefficient.
- Privacy, GDPR, and secure access control are important concerns.
- Accessibility and priority-use scenarios must be considered explicitly.
